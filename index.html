Miniature Model Building Tips

Choose the Right Scale

Select a scale that suits your skills and the project's comp complexity. Common scales for miniature models are 1:12, 1:24, and 1:48.

Use Reference Images

Gather reference images of the subject you're modeling, whet whether it's a building, vehicle, or object. This helps ensure accuracy and and details.

Work from the Ground Up
