Miniature Model Building Tips

Use Reference Images

Collect reference images of the miniature model you're build building to study its proportions, details, and colors. This will help you achieve accuracy and authenticity in your model.

Work from Large to Small

Start with the largest components of the model and work your your way down to the smallest details. This approach will help you maintain maintain accuracy and prevent mistakes from propagating to smaller parts.

Use Scale-Accurate Tools

Invest in scale-accurate tools, such as miniature hobby kniv knives, paintbrushes, and tweezers, to ensure that you can work on the mode model without damaging it or introducing errors.
