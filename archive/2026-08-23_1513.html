1

Use a flat, straightedge saw for making precise cuts on scale models

A good example is the Tamiya Hobby Knife saw or similar equivalent. This type of saw is ideal for making long, straight cuts without causing the model to warp or bind during the cutting process.

2

Select the correct grit file for the scale model material

For plastics, a fine-toothed file (1/0 or finer) is recommended, while for metals, a coarse-toothed file (1/4 or coarser) is better suited. This helps prevent clogging and ensures smooth, efficient cutting.

3

Use a flat, round, or half-round sanding file for smoothing scale model edges

These types of files are perfect for removing small imperfections and achieving a smooth finish, especially when working with complex shapes or curves.

4

Use a precision cutting tool, like a hobby knife or a X-Acto knife, for fine detail work

These tools are ideal for making delicate cuts, such as cutting small holes, notches, or fine lines. They are also useful for sculpting and shaping small details.

5

Regularly maintain your scale model tools to prevent wear and tear

Clean and sharpen your tools regularly to prevent them from becoming dull or rusty. This will ensure they continue to perform well and maintain the accuracy and precision required for scale model work.
