Fixing Silvered Decals After They Have Dried

Silvered decals happen when air is trapped under the decal. To fix after drying: apply Mr. Mark Softer generously over the decal, wait 10 minutes, then press gently with a damp cotton swab from the center outward to force air bubbles out. Repeat twice more with fresh solution. If silvering persists, carefully slit the decal film with a new blade in a crosshatch pattern, apply setting solution again, and press flat. Once fully settled, seal with a gloss coat.

Removing Old Paint from Kit Parts

To remove old paint from kit parts, use Citadel's 'Army Painter' brush-on primer remover. Apply with a soft brush or cotton swab, wait 15-30 minutes, then scrub gently with an old toothbrush. Rinse under warm water and allow parts to dry thoroughly before repainting.

Fixing Broken Photo-Etch Parts

Repair broken photo-etch parts using CA (Cyanoacrylate) glue. Apply a small amount on the inside of the part, join the broken edges together, and hold in place for 30 seconds. Once bonded, use a needle file or dental pick to remove excess glue. Seal with Vallejo Acrylic Paint Thinner to remove any remaining CA fumes.

Recovering Clogged Airbrushes

To recover a clogged airbrush, disassemble and soak the nozzle, needle, and cap in Vallejo Airbrush Thinner for 30 minutes. Use a soft brush to scrub gently, then reassemble and test spray with Vallejo Airbrush Thinner until clear. Backflush with thinner to ensure nozzle is clear.

Repairing Warped Parts

Repair warped kit parts by immersing them in warm water for 10 minutes, then gently bend back into shape. Allow to dry completely in the reshaped position. For extra support, use thin strips of masking tape to hold shape during drying. Severe warping may require professional repair or replacement.
